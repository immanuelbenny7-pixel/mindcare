1. INTRODUCTION & CORE PHILOSOPHY
================================================================================

What MindCare Is:
MindCare is a soothing, distraction-free digital space created to help you slow 
down, check in with yourself, and observe natural patterns in your mood, sleep, 
and daily stress levels. It pairs intuitive self-reflection tools with an 
empathetic AI assistant named Prakash.

What MindCare Is NOT (Critical Boundaries):
1. NOT A MEDICAL OR CLINICAL PRODUCT: MindCare is designed strictly for personal 
   wellness and self-reflection. It does NOT diagnose mental health conditions, 
   does NOT provide therapy or medical treatments, and CANNOT substitute for a 
   licensed doctor, psychiatrist, psychologist, or certified therapist.
2. NOT AN EMERGENCY RESPONDER: Prakash and MindCare are automated software systems. 
   They are not monitored by humans or emergency services. If you or someone you 
   know is in immediate crisis, contact emergency services (e.g. 988 in US/Canada, 
   14416 in India, 111/999 in UK).
3. NO INFERRED DIAGNOSES: Facial expression estimation cannot read minds or 
   detect true emotions. It is provided only as a tentative, non-binding guess 
   of visible features that you can freely accept, modify, or ignore.

Privacy-First Architecture:
- Your reflection logs, mood entries, sleep journal, and preferences are stored 
  locally on your own device in your web browser's storage (LocalStorage).
- Photos uploaded for facial expression guessing are processed strictly in-memory 
  by Google Gemini on the server and are NEVER saved to disk or database.
- Your Gemini API key is kept safely on the server and is never exposed to the 
  browser or frontend code.

================================================================================

Starting the Server:
1. Open PowerShell or Command Prompt in the project folder.
2. Ensure dependencies are installed and the server is running:
     npm start

Accessing MindCare:
Open your preferred web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Safari, Firefox) 
and navigate to:
     http://localhost:3001

The interface adapts smoothly to all screens: desktop monitors, laptops, 
tablets, and mobile smartphones.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
Introduces you to MindCare's gentle approach, establishes non-medical boundaries, 
and gives you granular control over AI features before any data is processed.

Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. On your first visit, the Onboarding Modal appears automatically.
2. Step 1 (Welcome & Purpose): Read about what MindCare provides and its non-medical

4. Step 3 (Your Choices):

FEATURE 2: HOME DASHBOARD & PERSONALIZED RECOMMENDATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
Serves as your daily grounding home base. Greets you according to the time of day 
and presents transparent, personalized exercise recommendations.

Components on the Dashboard:
1. Welcoming Hero Banner:
   - Displays a dynamic greeting (Good morning / afternoon / evening).
   - Core question: "What are you noticing today?"
   - Three quick-action buttons:
     * "Log My Mood" -> Opens the Mood Check-in screen.
     * "Reflect with Prakash" -> Opens the AI companion conversation.
     * "Try an Exercise" -> Opens the restorative exercise library.

2. "Suggested For You" Recommendation Cards:
   - MindCare evaluates your latest self-reported logs (such as elevated stress, 
     restless sleep, low energy, or calm feelings) and selects suitable exercises.
   - Transparent Rationale: Every card states why it was recommended, for example:
     "Suggested because you logged restless sleep last night."
   - Feedback & Controls:
     * Click the "X" button to dismiss a suggestion you don't want.
     * Click the Thumbs Up (Helpful) or Thumbs Down (Not Helpful) icons to help 
       the recommendation engine learn your preferences.
     * Click "Begin" to launch the guided interactive exercise immediately.

3. "At A Glance" Summary Cards:
   - Displays your most recent Mood Check-in (emoji, label, intensity, and notes).
   - Displays your most recent Sleep Log (hours slept, quality, energy, and stress).

FEATURE 3: DAILY MOOD CHECK-IN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
Allows you to record what you are feeling in a safe, nonjudgmental format without 
labels like "good" or "bad".

Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Navigate to the "Mood Log" tab in the top navigation bar.
2. Step 1 (Select Mood): Click on one of the 10 mood descriptors:
   - Peaceful   (🌿 Centered, calm, and present)
   - Content    (☀️ Satisfied and comfortable with where things are)
   - Joyful     (✨ Lightness, gratitude, or uplifting energy)
   - Reflective (🌊 Pensive, noticing your inner world)
   - Energized  (⚡ Active, engaged, and motivated)
   - Fatigued   (🌙 Low physical or mental energy; drained)
   - Anxious    (🍃 Restless mind or anticipatory worries)
   - Stressed   (🌪️ Feeling pressured, tight shoulders, or stretched thin)
   - Overwhelmed(🌧️ Too much stimulus or demands piling on)
   - Low / Flat (🍂 Subdued, quiet, emotional heaviness)
3. Step 2 (Intensity Slider): Drag the slider from 1 (Subtle whisper) to 10 
   (Very pronounced) to record how strongly you feel this state.
4. Step 3 (Context Tags): Select any tags that apply to your day (Work, Family, 
   Sleep, Outdoors, Quiet Time, etc.). You can also type your own custom tag 
   and click the "+" button.
5. Step 4 (Personal Notes): Optionally type anything you want to remember about 
   what is happening in your body or mind.
6. Click "Save Check-In". A confirmation alert will appear, and your entry will 
   be saved to your private local storage.

Optional Facial Expression Observation (AI Vision):
1. Click the "Optional: Photo expression guess" button at the top right of the 
   Mood form.
2. Review the uncertainty notice: Facial expressions do not represent true inner 
   emotions and computers can only guess visible facial muscles.
3. Check the consent box: "I understand this is an uncertain expression guess..."
4. Upload or capture a photo (JPG/PNG).
5. Click "Analyze Visible Expression". Gemini AI examines only surface features 
   (such as a slight smile or furrowed brow) and provides a tentative suggestion.
6. Review the result:
   - Click "Accept as Starting Mood" to apply it to your form.
   - Click "Keep My Own Choice" to discard the AI guess.
   - Note: The image is processed in memory and discarded immediately. It is 
     NEVER saved to any server disk or database.

FEATURE 4: SLEEP & WELLBEING LOG
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
Allows you to record your nightly rest, morning energy, and physical tension to 
discover personal rest cycles over time.

Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Navigate to the "Sleep & Rest" tab.
2. In the "Log Last Night's Rest" form:
   - Bedtime: Select the time you turned off lights (e.g., 23:00).
   - Wake Time: Select the time you got out of bed (e.g., 07:00).
   - Estimated Rest: MindCare calculates your sleep hours automatically (e.g., 8.0 hrs).
3. Select Quality of Rest:
   - Deep & Restorative (✨ Woke refreshed, uninterrupted)
   - Restful            (🌿 Solid sleep, woke naturally)
   - Fair / Okay        (🌤️ Average rest, minor awakenings)
   - Restless           (🌀 Tossed and turned, racing thoughts)
   - Interrupted        (⚡ Woke multiple times throughout the night)
4. Set Morning Energy: Slide from 1 (Drained) to 5 (Vibrant).
5. Set Stress / Tension: Slide from 1 (Calm) to 5 (Elevated).
6. Optional Notes: Record anything relevant (e.g. "Read for 15 mins; avoided screens").
7. Click "Save Sleep Log".
8. Managing Logs: Your recent entries appear in the right-hand column. You can delete 
   any entry by clicking the trash icon.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:

FEATURE 6: EXERCISE LIBRARY & GUIDED INTERACTIVE PRACTICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
A collection of evidence-informed, low-risk practices to center your breathing, 
ground your senses, or gently stretch your body whenever you need a pause.

Categories Available:
- Breathing: Paced rhythmic breathing to regulate the nervous system.
- Grounding: Sensory reconnection techniques for racing thoughts or anxiety.
- Gentle Movement: Low-effort neck, shoulder, and walking exercises.
- Journaling: Structured writing prompts for unburdening mental clutter.
- Sleep Wind-Down: Calming practices designed for evening bedtime routines.

Interactive Guides & Tools:
1. Box Breathing (4-4-4-4):
   - Features an animated, pulsating breathing circle.
   - Guides you through: Inhale for 4s -> Hold for 4s -> Exhale for 4s -> Rest for 4s.
   - Includes real-time countdown timer and step instructions.
2. 4-7-8 Calming Breath:
   - Smooth animated pacer: Inhale 4s -> Hold 7s -> Slow whoosh exhale 8s.
3. 5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding:
   - Step-by-step interactive countdown:
     * 5 things you can see
     * 4 things you can physically feel
     * 3 things you can hear
     * 2 things you can smell
     * 1 thing you can taste
4. Gentle Neck & Shoulder Release:
   - Seated mobility sequence with countdown timers for shoulder rolls and side tilts.
5. Evening Thought Unburdening:
   - Prompts you to brain dump lingering thoughts into a private scratchpad, 
     park unresolved items for tomorrow, and close with a compassionate note.
6. Completion & Helpfulness Feedback:
   - When finished, a gentle confetti celebration acknowledges your pause.
   - You are asked: "Did this exercise feel helpful today?" (Yes / Not quite). 
     Your answer dynamically informs future personalized suggestions on your Dashboard.
   - You can pause or exit any exercise at any time without penalty.

FEATURE 7: PRAKASH AI REFLECTION COMPANION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
Prakash is your dedicated AI companion for gentle, nonjudgmental reflection. 
Prakash listens first, validates your feelings, asks one question at a time, 
and offers practical wellness exercises.

How to Interact with Prakash:
1. Navigate to the "Prakash AI" tab.
2. Sharing Check-In Context (Transparent Toggle):
   - In the top header of the chat, locate the "Logs context" button.
   - Active (Eye icon): Prakash can see your most recent mood descriptor (e.g. 
     Anxious, 7/10) and last sleep duration to provide more relevant thoughts. 
     Whenever Prakash references this, a clear badge appears below the message.
   - Hidden (Eye Off icon): Prakash converses without accessing any of your logs. 
     You can toggle this off at any time.
3. Conversational Style:
   - Prakash speaks with warmth, calm empathy, and brevity (no long lectures).
   - Prakash asks only ONE question at a time so you never feel overwhelmed.
   - Quick Starters: If you are unsure what to say, click any of the starter 
     chips below the chat (e.g. "I'm having trouble unwinding", "My thoughts feel 
     scattered").
4. Interactive Exercise Suggestions:
   - If Prakash suggests an activity (like Box Breathing or Sensory Grounding), 
     an interactive button appears directly inside the chat message. Clicking 
     it opens the guided exercise player immediately.
5. Freedom to Decline:
   - You can say "no", "not now", or "I just want to vent". Prakash will never 
     pressure, guilt, or scold you.
6. Offline / Fallback Resilience:
   - If the Gemini API experiences network delays or rate limits, Prakash 
     automatically provides local reflection replies so you are never left waiting.

FEATURE 8: CRISIS PROTOCOL & VERIFIED EMERGENCY SUPPORT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
Ensures immediate access to human help when users experience acute emotional 
distress, danger, or thoughts of self-harm.

How Safety is Enforced:
1. Crisis Keyword Interception: If a message mentions self-harm, ending life, or 
   severe distress, Prakash immediately pauses conversational banter, responds with 
   warm compassion, and displays verified crisis resources.
2. The Crisis Support Modal:
   - Accessible anytime from the top navigation bar by clicking "Crisis Support".
   - Verified 24/7 Contacts:
     * United States & Canada: 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (Call or Text 988)
     * India: Tele-MANAS National Helpline (Call 14416 or 1800-891-4416)
     * United Kingdom: NHS Mental Health Services (Call 111, or 999 for emergencies)
     * International: Direct links to Find A Helpline (https://findahelpline.com) 
       and Befrienders Worldwide (https://www.befrienders.org).
3. Clear Reminder: MindCare is software, not human care. Support from these hotlines 
   is free, confidential, and staffed 24/7 by trained human counselors.

5. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ) & TECHNICAL DETAILS
================================================================================

Q1: Does anyone else see my mood notes, sleep logs, or chat messages?
A: No. All reflection records are saved on your local device in your browser's 
   LocalStorage. When chatting with Prakash or analyzing photos, requests are 
   transmitted via secure encrypted server proxy to Google Gemini without 
   retaining copies on disk.

Q2: Can I use MindCare without an internet connection or without AI?
A: Yes. All manual mood check-ins, sleep logging, timeline tracking, SVG charts, 
   data export, and the entire interactive exercise library work 100% locally 
   without internet or AI API keys. If the Gemini API key is missing or offline, 
   Prakash gracefully switches to compassionate local reflection responses.

Q3: Can a photo diagnose my depression, anxiety, or true mood?
A: Absolutely not. Facial expressions are complex social and physical movements 
   that often differ from internal emotional reality. MindCare treats all photo 
   estimations as tentative, surface-level guesses and gives you complete authority 
   to override or discard them.

Q4: Where is the Gemini API key configured?
A: It is configured in the root .env file on the server (GEMINI_API_KEY). It is 
   never exposed to frontend code or client browser inspection.

Q5: How do I export or backup my data?
A: Go to either the "Insights" tab or the "Settings" tab and click "Export (JSON)" 
   or "Download All Data (JSON)". Your browser will download a clean JSON file 
   containing your check-in history, sleep entries, and preferences.

Q6: How do I clear all my data?
A: Go to "Settings" and click "Permanently Delete All Data". Confirm the prompt, 
   and all stored records will be completely erased from your device.
